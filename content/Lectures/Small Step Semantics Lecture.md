## Synopsis


The system we will talk about today is called *small-step semantics*, also
called *transition semantics*, or even *operational semantics*. The idea with this
semantics is that we model what one step of computation looks like, and use that
to describe the meaning of a program.

One very important concept that comes up with this form is the *Church-Rosser theorem*.
