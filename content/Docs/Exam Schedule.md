You can register for exams at [Prairietest](https://prairietest.org).  Some guidelines:
- Bad Things$^{tm}$ tend to happen to people who register for the last possible time slot.  Leave yourself some margin if you can.
- Similarly, don't wait too long to register for a slot.  The CBTF can fill up.
- **If you know you are going to miss the exam appointment, cancel or reschedule it on Prairietest.**
- **If you missed the exam, email [[Mattox Beckman]] ASAP to make arrangements.**
