## Why this is important

Continuations allow us to manipulate the flow of a program directly.  These are used in languages that lack threading or
exceptions to implement them, in compilers as part of an optimization sequence, and in GUI application, where they are
called "callbacks".

## Notes
- A program transformed to use continuations is said to be in *continuation passing style*, **not** *continuous style*.
