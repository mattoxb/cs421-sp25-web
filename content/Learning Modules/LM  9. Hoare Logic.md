## What is it?

Hoare Logic, named after logician Tony Hoare, is a systematic way of representing the effect a program has on the properties of the system, particularly the variables.
## Why this is important 

Sometimes you want to be able to demonstrate that a program does what you wanted it to do, i.e., that it's *correct*.  The Hoare Semantics is a powerful way to do this.
