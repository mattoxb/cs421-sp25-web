The final exam will cover everything that was on the three midterm exams.

Each midterm exam was broken into various zones.  These zones are repeated on the final.  If you attempt a zone on the final exam and it improves over the score you got on that particular zone, we will replace it.  If your score was lower, we will replace it by the average.

If you are happy with your course grade at the end of the course, you may skip the final.
